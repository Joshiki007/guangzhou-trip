🍽️ Day 1 Lunch / Food Ideas (Xiguan Area)

  • Yinji Changfen (银记肠粉): A local staple since the 50s. Great for fast, cheap, delicate soy sauce beef rice noodle rolls and Tingzai (boat) porridge.
  • Panxi Restaurant (泮溪酒家): Near Pantang Village. Famous for traditional dim sum inside a beautiful classical Lingnan garden setting.
  • Snack Stalls on Shangxiajiu: Perfect for evening grazing (fish balls, radish offal, double-skin milk).

Day 1: Full Detail Plan

1. 8:30 AM: Leave Hotel & Transit

Option A (Best): Grab a DiDi right outside Jiangwan Yihao straight to Nanyue King Museum entrance (~12 mins | ~¥15).
Option B (Metro - Direct): Walk 12 minutes west along the Pearl River promenade to Haizhu Square Station, then take Line 2 direct north for 3 stops to Yuexiu Park Station (Exit E) with zero transfers.

2. 9:00 AM: Nanyue King Museum (Tomb Area)

Cost: ¥10 | Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Closed Mondays).
Start your morning deep underground. This is the authentic 2,000-year-old tomb of the Nanyue King, discovered fully intact in the 1980s. You will walk down into the actual excavated stone burial chambers. Spend about 2 hours exploring the tomb and the incredible jade burial suits on display.

3. 11:00 AM: Temple of the Six Banyan Trees (Optional)

Cost: Free / ~¥5 | Hours: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM.
Take Line 2 south two stops to Gongyuanqian Station (Exit I2). Walk 5 minutes to view the multi-colored 57-meter Flower Pagoda and Buddha halls before heading to Chen Clan Ancestral Hall.

4. 11:50 AM: Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

Cost: ¥10 | Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Closed Tuesdays).
From Gongyuanqian Station, take Line 1 west two stops to Chen Clan Academy Station. This 19th-century academic complex is the absolute pinnacle of Lingnan architecture. You'll spend about an hour admiring the impossibly intricate wood, brick, and ivory carvings that line every roof and beam.

5. 12:45 PM: Lunch Time

Transit: Walk toward Liwan District.
Head toward your next destination (Shamian/Liwan) and decide on a lunch spot along the way. See the recommendations block at the bottom of the Day 1 tab for local ideas.

6. 2:00 PM: Shamian Island

Cost: Free | Transit: Line 1 to Huangsha.
Hop back on Line 1 for two stops to Huangsha Station. Walk across the short pedestrian bridge to Shamian Island. This former British and French concession is a peaceful, pedestrian-only sandbank lined with massive banyan trees and restored 19th-century European mansions. Grab a coffee and spend an hour walking the shaded avenues.

7. 3:30 PM: Pantang Ancient Village & Liwan Old Town

Cost: Free | Transit: 15-min walk or quick DiDi.
Head north from Shamian into the heart of the Liwan District. This is "Old Guangzhou." Walk through Pantang Ancient Village, which perfectly preserves its Qing Dynasty street layout. Stroll along the Lizhiwan Creek to see the traditional Xiguan Mansions (homes of wealthy merchants from the 1800s) with their distinctive "Tanglong" sliding wooden doors.

8. 6:00 PM: Yongqing Fang & Shangxiajiu

Cost: Free | Transit: Walk.
As the sun sets, walk into Yongqing Fang. It transforms at night—especially the famous "Moon in the Bridge" landscape which lights up and looks incredibly romantic. After taking photos, walk 10 minutes east to Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street. It’s loud, neon-lit, and packed with historic Cantonese snack stalls. Have a casual dinner here.

9. 8:30 PM: Return to Hotel

Transit: Line 6 or DiDi.
Walk to the nearby Cultural Park Station and take Line 6 straight back to Beijing Lu Station, or simply grab a DiDi directly back to your hotel on Yanjiang Middle Road.

Day 2: Full Detail Plan

1. 9:30 AM: Leave Hotel & Transit to CBD

Option A (Best): Take a DiDi directly from Jiangwan Yihao to Huacheng Square (~15 mins | ~¥20). Drops you right above ground in the central plaza and avoids underground morning rush-hour crowds.
Option B (Metro): Walk to Beijing Lu Station ➔ Line 6 east to Dongshankou ➔ Transfer to Line 1/3 toward Zhujiang New Town.

2. 9:45 AM: Zhujiang New Town & Huacheng Square

Cost: Free | Hours: Open 24/7.
Walk through Guangzhou’s premier civic axis. Stroll the green boulevard surrounded by ultra-modern landmarks, including the Zaha Hadid-designed Guangzhou Opera House, Guangzhou Library, and the 530m CTF Finance Centre tower.

3. 10:30 AM: Guangdong Museum

Cost: Free (WeChat Reservation + Passport Required) | Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Closed Mondays).
Cross the plaza into this architectural spectacle designed like an ancient Chinese lacquer box. Explore four floors of Cantonese wood carvings, Lingnan historical jade, porcelain, and natural history exhibits. Spend about 2 hours inside.

4. 1:00 PM: CBD Lunch Break

Transit: Walk.
Head underground into Mall of the World (花城汇) directly beneath the plaza for fast, air-conditioned local and international dining options, or walk to K11 Art Mall / IFC Mall for modern fusion dining and cafes.

5. 2:30 PM: Haixinsha Asian Games Park

Cost: Free | Transit: 10-min walk south to riverbank.
Walk south to the pedestrian island where the 2010 Asian Games opening ceremony was held. This riverside park provides the single best unobstructed, ground-to-tip photo angle of Canton Tower directly across the water, as well as views of the Haixin Pedestrian Bridge.

6. 4:30 PM: Canton Tower (Golden Hour & Sunset)

Cost: ~¥150 – ¥398 | Transit: APM Line (1 stop underground ~3 mins).
Take the automated APM light rail from Haixinsha Station 1 stop under the river into Canton Tower Station. Ascend to the observation decks (~433m–450m). Arriving at 4:30 PM allows you to clear security, reach the top, and experience the transition from daytime city views to sunset (around 5:45 PM) and the full night skyline lighting up.

7. 7:30 PM: Pearl River Night Cruise

Cost: ~¥80 – ¥150 | Transit: Walk to Canton Tower Fortune Pier.
Board a 60-to-90-minute evening cruise to see Liede Bridge, Haizhu Bridge, and the illuminated skyline from the water.
Crucial Shortcut: Book a boarding ticket that specifies Tianzi Wharf (天字码头) as the drop-off point. When the boat docks, you step right off onto your hotel’s street without queuing for post-tour taxis!

Day 3: Full Detail Plan

1. 9:00 AM: Baiyun Mountain

Cost: ¥5 Entry (Cable car extra) | Transit: DiDi Direct to South Gate Cable Car (~25 mins | ~¥30).
Why DiDi is best: Metro Line 2 drops you 1.5 km away from the actual cable car station. Taking a DiDi directly to the Baiyun Mountain South Gate (白云山南门) drops you right at the ticket window. Take the cable car up to Moxing Peak for panoramic views over Guangzhou, and walk down through lush botanical trails and tea gardens.

2. 1:00 PM: Lunch Break & Travel to Dongshankou

Transit: DiDi or Metro Line 1 / Line 6 to Dongshankou Station.
Travel south into the historic Dongshankou neighborhood. Find a trendy cafe, boutique bistro, or artisanal bakery inside one of the repurposed 1920s villas for a relaxed lunch.

3. 2:00 PM: Dongshankou Art District

Cost: Free | Transit: Walk.
Explore "Old Money Guangzhou." Dongshankou is famous for its preserved 1920s Western-style red-brick mansions built by returned overseas Chinese. Today, the narrow residential alleys house independent designer boutiques, contemporary art galleries, coffee shops, and photography spots.

4. 4:30 PM: Sacred Heart Cathedral (Shishi)

Cost: Free | Transit: Metro Line 6 to Yide Lu Station + 5-min walk.
Take Line 6 straight from Dongshankou west to Yide Lu Station. Also known as the "Stone House," this breathtaking 19th-century Gothic Revival cathedral was built entirely from granite stone by French architects.
Important Note: The gates close promptly to tourists at 5:00 PM (17:00), so ensure you arrive before 4:30 PM to explore the interior vaulted ceilings and stained glass windows!
